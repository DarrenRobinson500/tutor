This is a maths tutoring platform for K-10 students (NSW curriculum). Here's what it does:

  For Tutors
  - Create and manage question templates with randomised parameters, so students never see the same question twice
  - Templates support LaTeX rendering, diagrams, multi-step questions, and various answer formats
  - Organise questions by skill, difficulty (easy/medium/hard), and year level
  - Track which parts of the syllabus have template coverage via a Skills Matrix
  - Attach Knowledge items (reference notes, diagrams) to questions to show students when they answer incorrectly
  - Book students for weekly or one-off sessions

  For Students
  - Work through questions and get immediate feedback
  - See worked solutions and reference notes when they get something wrong
  - Take skills assessments that track which topics they've mastered
  - Enter answers in various formats — fractions, decimals, ratios, powers, prime factorisation

  For Parents
  - View how their child is progressing

  Admin/Reporting
  - PDF test reports
  - Past test history with per-skill results
  - Skills coverage dashboard showing validated templates across year levels and difficulties

  The platform handles the NSW K-10 maths syllabus specifically, with question difficulty graded across easy, medium, and hard tiers.
