Competition rules

What you need to know

Read before you register. Questions? contact@marketmakers.edu.au

Open to all currently enrolled undergraduate and postgraduate students at participating universities. Students on academic probation are ineligible. Staff, faculty, and direct family members of judges or business owners are also ineligible.

The following marketing tactics are permitted within competition rules:

  • Organic social (Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook, X)
  • Paid social advertising
  • Content marketing
  • Email marketing
  • Campus guerrilla marketing (with uni approval)
  • Influencer outreach
  • SEO
  • Community events & pop-ups
  • Referral programmes

The following are strictly prohibited and may result in immediate disqualification:

  • Purchasing fake followers, reviews, or engagement
  • Spam in violation of the Spam Act 2003
  • False or misleading claims
  • Unlicensed stock images
  • Accessing business customer data without consent
  • Cold calling without consent
  • Targeting under-18s for Forever Bloom
  • AI-generated content presented as original human work without disclosure
